Known limitation retained and reported
--------------------------------------
Severe-disease FCL severity is compressed downward despite strong overall
specificity and paper-style pHR. This was investigated with reconstruction-stage,
coverage-provenance, projection-distance and covered-thickness diagnostics.
No tested morphology/postprocessing change improved severe FCL without materially
damaging human-zero specificity; therefore no v17 morphology rule was adopted.
